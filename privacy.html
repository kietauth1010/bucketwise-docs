Data security & privacy

What Bucketwise accesses in your Jira site, what it stores, and what it does with either.

Effective September 26, 2026

Summary

Bucketwise is an Atlassian Forge app built for Jira Software. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, and does not operate any servers, databases, or infrastructure of its own outside that platform.

Data storedNone
Third-party sharingNone
InfrastructureAtlassian Forge only
External network callsNone

What data Bucketwise accesses

When installed on a Jira site, Bucketwise reads and writes only the Jira Software data needed to run a bucket estimation session:

  • Issue details — key, summary, description preview, issue type, and current Story Points value — for issues in the project's backlog, board, or sprints.
  • Board and sprint configuration, including which custom field is used for Story Points.
  • Project metadata needed to scope the above.

Bucketwise writes back only to the Story Points field of issues the facilitator explicitly chooses to save.

What Bucketwise stores

Bucketwise stores no data of its own. Every time the board is opened, it is rebuilt fresh from your Jira site's live data.

No draft, no cache, no tracking

There is no database, draft, or user-tracking record kept between sessions, and no personal data — names, emails, or account identifiers — is retained after a request completes.

Data sharing with third parties

Bucketwise does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. All data exchanged by the app stays between your Jira site and Atlassian's own Forge runtime — there are no external API calls, analytics services, or logging destinations outside Atlassian's platform.

Encryption and infrastructure

Bucketwise inherits its infrastructure security directly from Atlassian Forge, including encryption in transit and at rest, and Atlassian's own compliance certifications for the underlying platform. Tessera Labs does not manage or have access to any separate infrastructure for this app.

Your rights

Because Bucketwise retains no personal data after a request completes, there is no stored personal data to access, export, or erase. Uninstalling the app immediately removes its access to your Jira site's data.

Changes to this statement

If Bucketwise's data handling changes in a future version, this statement will be updated and the effective date above will reflect the change.
